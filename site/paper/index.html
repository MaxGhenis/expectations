What do forecasters say they don't know?

Elicited macroeconomic uncertainty across the US and euro-area Surveys of Professional Forecasters, 1968–2026: every reported histogram pooled by the law of total variance, decomposed into stated individual uncertainty and disagreement, calibrated against realized outcomes, and scored (CRPS, pinball) against no-lookahead benchmarks.
